# DESIGN SYSTEM: POLITEKNIK SINGLE SIGN-ON (SSO) HUB

## Aesthetic Direction: "Refined Academic Craft"
An authoritative, clean, and utilitarian design inspired by modern university enterprise portals (MIT, Oxford, Stanford, NUS) combined with Stripe-level interface polish.

### 1. Typography
- **Primary Body / UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or system geometric sans with optical kerning.
- **Editorial Headings**: Refined serifs or heavy geometric sans with tight tracking (`tracking-tight`).
- **Codes, Tokens, & Metrics**: Tabular figures in monospaced font (`font-mono`).

### 2. Palette (Muted Institutional Authority)
- **Primary Navy**: `#0f172a` (Slate 900) and `#1e3a8a` (Collegiate Blue 900)
- **Backgrounds**: Crisp Warm White `#ffffff`, Clean Slate Canvas `#f8fafc`, subtle contrast `#f1f5f9`
- **Borders & Dividers**: Crisp, precise 1px lines `#e2e8f0` (never blurry heavy drop-shadows)
- **Service Signatures**:
  - **Akademik (SIAKAD)**: Deep Slate & Indigo (`#3730a3`)
  - **E-Learning (LMS)**: Muted Forest & Emerald (`#065f46`)
  - **Perpustakaan (DigiLib)**: Warm Amber & Chestnut (`#92400e`)

### 3. Anti-AI Slop Rules
1. No multi-colored glowing radial gradient blobs.
2. No repetitive centered 3-card columns with giant colored circle icons.
3. No excessive uppercase badge pills on every single text element.
4. Robust image fallbacks (`onerror`) to prevent broken avatar rendering.
5. High information utility: real schedules, academic metrics, and actionable workflows.
