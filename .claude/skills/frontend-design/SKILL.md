# Frontend Design & Taste Guidelines (Anti-Slop Craft)

This skill enforces intentional, high-craft art direction and rejects generic AI boilerplate.

## 1. Anti-Pattern Rejections (Avoid AI Slop)
- **NO Generic Glowing Gradients**: Never slap centered `from-purple-600 via-indigo-600 to-blue-500` blobs without structural purpose.
- **NO Repetitive 3-Card Columns**: Do not force every feature into an identical centered grid of 3 rounded rectangles with a colored circle icon.
- **NO Pill/Badge Overuse**: Do not sprinkle uppercase pill badges (`bg-indigo-100 text-indigo-700 font-bold`) above every single heading.
- **NO Default Inter/Roboto Monotony**: Mix purposeful serif or editorial sans-serif headers with clean, tabular-friendly body typography.
- **NO Low-Density Fluff**: Interfaces for enterprise, academic, and administrative tools must have functional information density, clear hierarchy, and actual utility.

## 2. Institutional Academic Craft (Politeknik SSO Aesthetic)
- **Palette**: Deep collegiate navy (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`, `#1e3a8a`), crisp warm stone/slate (`#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`), warm white backgrounds, subtle border hairlines (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Typography**: High contrast, authoritative headings, precise optical alignment, monospace data fields (`font-mono`) for codes and tokens.
- **Component Polish**:
  - Crisp 1px borders (`border-slate-200/80`) instead of heavy artificial drop-shadows.
  - Tactile micro-interactions (subtle hover state shifts, clean focus rings).
  - Data density with tabular figures for numbers, dates, and grades.
